Before we begin

A few things
you might wonder.

How do you connect project work to business goals?+

Start with the change you want to see: a clearer customer journey, a more connected workflow, or better visibility over delivery. We can then discuss the requirements, priorities, and evidence that would help you judge whether the work meets that need.

Can we discuss project management or technical leadership?+

Yes. Alongside development, my experience includes requirements analysis, implementation planning, team coordination, and IT management. Share the project’s current stage and the support you need so we can discuss the fit.

What kind of projects can we work on?+

Business websites, campaign landing pages, custom web applications, admin dashboards, and third-party integrations. Share your goals and I can help identify the right approach.

How does a project start?+

Send a short overview by email or WhatsApp: what you want to build, who it is for, and any requirements you already have. We can discuss the scope and next steps from there.

Can you improve an existing website?+

Yes. I work on redesigns, performance improvements, new features, and integrations for existing websites as well as new builds.

How long will it take, and what will it cost?+

Both depend on the scope, functionality, and content. After discussing your requirements, we can establish a realistic timeline and estimate.

Can you connect my website to other tools?+

Yes. My experience includes Salesforce, Zoho, Odoo, REST APIs, and payment gateways, including Amazon Payment Gateway with Apple Pay.

Do you provide support after launch?+

Maintenance and ongoing support can be discussed as part of the project, including updates, bug fixes, and performance improvements.
